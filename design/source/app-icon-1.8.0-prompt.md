Do not fill the cat itself with blue. Keep the entire cat centered and visible, with comfortable blue breathing room around ears and sides suitable for rounded or circular OS icon clipping. No new objects, no text, no border, no white outside margin, no rounded corners baked in, no gradients, no 3D effects. Match the blue color and uneven hand-colored paper texture of the original. Deliver one square opaque image.
